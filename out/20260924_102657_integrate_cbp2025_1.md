## result
I have identified and verified the root cause of the massive `G5` regression. 

The original port plan requested adding `sr_sum` to `LSUM` within the `predict()` function. However, the plan author misunderstood where `LSUM` is evaluated. `LSUM` is re-zeroed and re-evaluated inside `predict_using_given_hist()`, meaning anything added in `predict()` is ignored and bypassed for the prediction. Even more importantly, during `update()`, TAGE's SC re-calls `predict_using_given_hist()` to re-compute `LSUM` for training. Because `sr_sum` was not saved or passed through at update time, the SC component trained heavily on broken data, resulting in a large regression in branch mispredictions (from 5.093 to 9.548). 

To fix this wiring fault, I have revised the `tage_h` hook in the port plan to allocate `sr_sum` inside the checkpointed `cbp_hist_t` struct, which successfully passes the correctly timed `sr_sum` payload into the update-time re-evaluation, fixing the training regression. I have tested this fix and confirmed it resolves the anomaly.
